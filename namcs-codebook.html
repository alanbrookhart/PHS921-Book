B NAMCS codebook

Both teaching cohorts carry many more columns than the ones we actually use in adjustment models (ns_covs and sta_covs in R/setup.R are short lists curated from a much wider survey). This appendix is the reference for the rest: a full variable-by-variable codebook, generated directly from the data rather than typed out by hand, so it will always match whatever is actually in the installed NAMCS package.

B.3 A note on the outcomes

As Chapter 2 mentioned, both cohorts are built from real NAMCS office visits, but their outcomes are simulated: incident_pud in ns, and the event-time variables (cv_time, death_time, loss_fu_time) that R/setup.R combines into event_time and cv_event in sta. Nothing about the covariates or the treatment assignment is invented; only the outcome is, generated from a data-generating process known exactly to whoever wrote the simulation. That is a deliberate choice for a teaching book, since it means every chapter’s estimate can in principle be checked against a right answer, which is a luxury no real analysis has. We analyze the cohorts here the way we would analyze real data, arguing for each assumption rather than looking the truth up, although the truth is there behind the file, which is what makes these two cohorts safe to learn on.
